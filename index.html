Sesiones y vías

Duración, esfuerzo percibido, ejercicios con series y carga, vías y bloques con grado, intentos y estilo de encadenamiento.

Forma y fatiga

A partir de la duración y el esfuerzo, CruxLog calcula la carga aguda y crónica y el balance de forma: sabes si hoy toca apretar o descansar.

Calendario y temporada

Vista de mes, semana y temporada. Planificaciones por bloques con las sesiones de cada día.

Entrenador de IA

Propone la sesión del día según el objetivo, el tiempo, el material y cómo te encuentras, y genera temporadas enteras.

Progreso y evaluación física

Evolución de grado y volumen, tests de fuerza de dedos y de tracción, y medidas corporales si quieres apuntarlas.

Climbing · training

The training log for climbers who train with a plan.

Log every session at the crag, the gym or the board, see how fitness and fatigue evolve, and plan your season without spreadsheets.

Open the web app Google Play

Sessions and routes

Duration, perceived effort, exercises with sets and load, routes and boulders with grade, attempts and ascent style.

Fitness and fatigue

From duration and effort, CruxLog computes acute and chronic load and your form balance: you know whether today is a day to push or to rest.

Calendar and season

Month, week and season views. Block-based plans with the sessions due each day.

AI coach

Suggests today's session from your goal, time, equipment and how you feel, and builds whole seasons.

Progress and testing

Grade and volume over time, finger-strength and pulling tests, and body measurements if you want to log them.

Communities and friends

Groups with chat, shared sessions and comments, a weekly leaderboard and private messages between friends.
